Is the van ready for the next trip? VanReady tells you at a glance, with a road-sign status
that turns green when everything's sorted. It's a maintenance logbook and pre-trip checker
for caravan, camper trailer and pop-top owners.

Before a trip it shows what needs doing: old tyres, services that will fall due on the way,
registration or insurance running out. Run the walk-around check as you hitch up, and any
problem you find stays on the Ready screen until it's fixed.

Log every service, repair and receipt with photos. VanReady reads tyre age from the
sidewall and fills in invoice details for you to check. Reminders for wheel bearings,
brakes, seals, gas, water and batteries come by kilometres or months, whichever is first.

It works offline with no account, and your records stay on your phone unless you share
them. Back everything up to one file, or export a service-history PDF for a buyer, a
warranty claim or an insurer. Made in Australia for caravanners, in kilometres and
kilograms.
