Meritly is a quiet place to record the good you do. Helping a neighbour, giving blood,
planting a tree, cooking for your family: each one becomes a merit, with a date, a short
description, the categories you choose and up to three photos.

Over time your merits become a story you can look back on. Search finds any of them in
seconds, and the insights show how your month and your year are going. Set a monthly or
yearly goal, and Meritly can remind you with a quote about doing good.

There are no ads and no accounts. Everything is stored on your phone, and you can back it
up to your own Google Drive if you choose.
