PayDay Mate is a pay calculator for Australian casual workers. Add your jobs and hourly
rates, log the shifts you've worked or been rostered for, and it shows what each one
should pay before the money reaches your account.

Weekend, public holiday, evening and overtime rates are applied automatically, using the
rates you set for each job. Public holidays are marked for your state. Tax is estimated
from the ATO's PAYG withholding formulas, taking into account residency, the tax-free
threshold, study loans, the Medicare levy and salary sacrifice. Super is shown alongside.

Each pay period is laid out like a payslip, so you can check it against the real one and
export it as a PDF or CSV. There is no account to create: your jobs and shifts are stored
on your phone.

Every figure is an estimate, not financial advice. The app is free and supported by ads.
