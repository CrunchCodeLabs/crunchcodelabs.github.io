VanReady is a caravan maintenance logbook and pre-trip checker for caravan, camper trailer and pop-top owners in Australia, made by CrunchCode Labs. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

## Your van, logbook and photos stay on your phone

Everything you enter in VanReady is stored only on your device: your van and tow vehicle details, tyre dates, service schedule, logbook entries, costs, notes, trips, checklists, registration and insurance dates, and the photos and invoices you attach. VanReady has no accounts and does not send this information to us or anyone else.

It leaves your phone only when you choose to send it:

*   **PDF exports.** A service history PDF goes only where you share it, such as an email to a buyer or your insurer.
*   **Backups.** "Back up now" makes a single file with your data and photos. It goes only where you save or send it, for example your Google Drive or your own email. Anyone who has that file can open it, so keep it somewhere private.
*   **Sharing the app.** "Share VanReady" sends a short invitation and a Google Play link through the app you pick. It contains none of your data.

Android may include the app's data in your device backup if you have Android backup turned on. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings.

## Camera and photos

VanReady asks for the camera or your photos only when you choose to photograph a tyre sidewall, an invoice or a job. Reading the tyre date code and the invoice text happens on your phone using Google's ML Kit text recognition; the images are not uploaded for this. Photos you keep are stored inside the app.

## Reminders

Service, registration, insurance and trip reminders are scheduled on your phone by Android. No reminder information is sent anywhere. You can turn reminders off in Settings or in your Android notification settings.

## Information collected by services the app uses

The app uses the following services, which collect some information automatically:

*   **Google AdMob** shows a small banner ad at the bottom of the Ready, Check and Logbook screens, and a short rewarded video ad when you choose to unlock a second van, a PDF export or an invoice read. It may collect your device's advertising ID, device and app information, and an approximate location based on your IP address, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before ads are personalised. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Ad-free for life removes all of these ads.
*   **Firebase Analytics** records how the app is used, such as which features are opened and whether a check was finished, together with device information and an app instance ID, so we can improve the app. These events never include your van's name, registration, business names, notes, costs or anything else you type.
*   **Firebase Crashlytics** collects crash reports, including device information and the state of the app when it crashed, so we can fix problems.
*   **Meta (Facebook) SDK** tells Meta when the app is installed and opened, and when you set up your first van, finish a check or buy Ad-free for life, together with your device's advertising ID and device information. We use this to measure and improve our ads for VanReady on Facebook and Instagram. No van details, notes, costs or anything you type are sent. Meta's use of this information is described in its [privacy policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/). You can limit it by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in your Android settings.
*   **Google Play** handles the optional "Ad-free for life" purchase, app updates and installs under Google's own terms. We receive confirmation that the purchase was made, not your payment details.

Google's use of this information is described in its [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and in [how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## What we don't do

*   We don't sell your information.
*   We don't ask for your name, address, email, licence or bank details.
*   We don't track your location. Trips and kilometres are only what you type in.
*   The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Deleting your information

Uninstalling VanReady, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes everything stored in the app. Backup files and PDFs you have shared are not affected; delete them where you saved them. To ask us to delete analytics or crash data linked to your device, write to the address below.
