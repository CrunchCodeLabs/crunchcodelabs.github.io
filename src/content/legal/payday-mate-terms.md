## 2. What the app is

PayDay Mate is a free tool that helps casual workers in Australia plan and record shifts and
estimate what they will be paid. From the jobs, rates and shifts you enter, it calculates
estimated gross pay, tax withheld, superannuation and take-home pay, and it can export a pay
period as a PDF or CSV file.

## 3. Estimates only, not financial advice

**Every figure the app shows is an estimate.** It is calculated from the information you
enter, the rate rules you set and the tax tables built into the app, and it may differ from
what you are actually paid. In particular:

*   Rate rules start from general defaults, not from your award or enterprise agreement.
    You are responsible for setting the rates that apply to you.
*   Tax is estimated using the Australian Taxation Office's PAYG withholding formulas for
    the selected financial year. Your employer's payroll system, your other income and your
    end-of-year tax assessment may produce different results.
*   Public holiday lists are provided for convenience and may not include local or
    regional holidays.

The app does not provide financial, tax, legal or employment advice, and it is not a
substitute for your payslip, your employer's records, the Fair Work Ombudsman or a registered
tax agent. If you think you have been underpaid, check your award and contact
[Fair Work](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/) or a qualified adviser.
