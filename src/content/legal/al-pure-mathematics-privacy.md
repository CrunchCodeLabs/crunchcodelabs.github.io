**A/L ශුද්ධ ගණිතය** (A/L Pure Mathematics)  
Android package `com.crunchcode.puremathematicssinhala`  
Effective 12 September 2026

**In short.** This app has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your bookmarks, quiz progress and reminder settings stay on your phone. The only information that leaves your device is an advertising identifier, read by Google AdMob, and only if you open the Daily Quiz. If you never open it, no advertising software ever starts.

## 1\. Who this is from

This app is published by **CrunchCode Labs**. If anything here is unclear, or you want to ask about your data, write to [crunchcodelabs@gmail.com](mailto:crunchcodelabs@gmail.com).

This policy covers the Android app named above, and nothing else.

## 2\. What stays on your device

The app stores a small amount of information locally so it can work the way you left it. None of it is sent to us, and we have no way of reading it:

*   Which sections you have bookmarked.
*   Your quiz progress: how many questions you have answered today, which questions you have seen recently, and how often you have answered each topic correctly or incorrectly. The last of these is what lets the quiz ask more about the topics you find hard.
*   Your reminder settings: whether reminders are on, the time, how often, and how many a day.

The formulas, diagrams and quiz questions are all packaged inside the app. Reading them needs no internet connection, and nothing about what you read is recorded or sent anywhere.

## 3\. Advertising

The Daily Quiz gives you three questions a day at no cost. If you want more on the same day, you may choose to watch a short video advertisement, which unlocks three more. That is the only advertising in the app: there are no banners, and nothing is shown to you unless you tap the button asking for it.

Advertisements are supplied by **Google AdMob**. To do that, Google reads your device's **advertising identifier** — a resettable ID that Android provides for this purpose — along with technical information such as your device type and approximate region. This is used to select and measure advertisements. We do not receive it, and we do not receive any report identifying you.

**This only happens inside the Daily Quiz.** The advertising software is started the first time you open that screen, not when the app launches. If you use the app only to look up formulas, to search, or to keep bookmarks, no advertising software runs and no advertising identifier is read.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services is described at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 4\. Your choices about advertising

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you are asked for your consent before any advertising software starts, using Google's consent form. You can change that choice at any time from **Privacy options** in the app's menu.

Everywhere in the world, Android lets you reset or delete your advertising identifier in _Settings → Privacy → Ads_. Deleting it stops personalised advertising across all your apps, not just this one.

You are never required to watch an advertisement. The three daily questions, and the whole formula reference, work without one.

## 5\. Backup

Android's own backup feature is enabled for this app, which is the default. That means the local information listed in section 2 may be copied to _your_ Google account's backup, in the same way Android backs up your other apps. It goes to your Google Drive, not to us, and you can turn it off in _Settings → Google → Backup_.

## 6\. Permissions, and why

*   **Internet** and **network state** — used only to fetch advertisements in the Daily Quiz. The app's content is local and needs neither.
*   **Advertising ID** — required by Google AdMob, as described above.
*   **Notifications** — used only for the revision reminder, and only asked for at the moment you switch reminders on. If you decline, everything else still works.

The app asks for no other permissions. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, files, or call history.

## 7\. Age

This app is made for students preparing for the Sri Lankan G.C.E. Advanced Level Combined Mathematics examination, who are typically 16 to 18 years old. It is not directed at children under 13, and it is not declared as a child-directed app.

## 8\. What this app does not do

*   It has no user accounts and asks for no name, email address or phone number.
*   It has no servers. There is no database anywhere holding your information.
*   It contains no analytics or crash-reporting software.
*   It does not track you across other apps or websites.
*   It does not sell information about you. We have none to sell.

## 9\. Deleting your information

Because everything is local, you remove it by removing it from your device: use _Settings → Apps → A/L ශුද්ධ ගණිතය → Storage → Clear data_, or simply uninstall the app. Nothing is left behind on our side, because nothing was ever sent there. If Android backup is on, also clear the app from your Google backup as described in section 5.

## 10\. Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling information differently, this page will be updated and the effective date at the top changed before that version is released. Significant changes will also be described in the Play Store release notes.
