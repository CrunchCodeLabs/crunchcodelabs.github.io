## 2. What the app is

VanReady helps owners of caravans, camper trailers and similar recreational trailers keep
track of maintenance and get ready for trips. It keeps a service logbook, works out when
services, tyres, registration and insurance are due, walks you through a pre-departure
checklist, reads tyre date codes and invoice text from photos, sends reminders, and exports
your service history as a PDF.

## 3. A reminder tool, not a safety inspection

**VanReady does not inspect your van and cannot tell you that it is safe or roadworthy.**
What it shows depends entirely on the information you enter and the schedules you set.
In particular:

*   Service intervals, tyre-age guidance and the default checklist are general starting
    points, not your manufacturer's requirements. Follow your van, axle, brake, tyre and
    appliance manufacturers' instructions and adjust the app to match them.
*   A "Ready to roll" result means only that nothing you have recorded is due or flagged.
    It is not a roadworthy, safety, gas or electrical certificate.
*   Tyre dates and invoice details read from photos can be wrong. Check every value the app
    fills in before you save it.
*   Weights shown are the figures you enter. They do not replace weighing your rig or
    knowing your vehicle's and van's legal limits.

You remain responsible for the safe condition, loading and towing of your vehicle and van,
and for complying with the road rules and registration requirements of each state and
territory you travel in. Have your van serviced and inspected by a qualified person.
